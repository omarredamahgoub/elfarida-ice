Technical Questions

What is the difference between refrigerants R404a and R507?+
Both are used in commercial freezing. The main difference: R507 is a binary blend (R125+R143a), thermally more stable and suitable for variable loads; R404a is a ternary blend (R125+R143a+R134a), cheaper and more widespread. Both have high GWP (>3900) and the industry is transitioning to eco-friendly alternatives such as R448A.
What is the difference between rack systems and split units?+
Rack systems centralise 3–12 compressors serving multiple rooms, saving up to 40% on electricity, enabling automatic Lead/Lag redundancy, unified maintenance, and heat recovery for free hot water. Split units have a lower capital cost but higher running costs, suitable for small facilities with fewer than 5 rooms.
When should an ammonia (NH3) system be used?+
R717 ammonia systems are suitable for large industrial facilities (>500 kW) such as poultry plants (>50 t/day), ice factories, large logistics warehouses (>10,000 m³), and large dairy operations. Advantages: COP > 4 (25% higher than Freon), zero environmental impact, 30% lower operating costs. They require advanced safety systems with Honeywell sensors.
What is a Cascade system and where is it used?+
A Cascade system operates in two stages: the first stage using R404a/R134a cools the second stage using CO2/R23 to achieve −70°C to −90°C. It is essential for: storing mRNA vaccines (Pfizer −70°C), blood plasma storage (−30°C), deep industrial freezing, scientific research laboratories, and advanced blast-freezing plants.
What is the ideal insulation panel thickness for a −25°C freezer room?+
For a −25°C freezer room in Saudi Arabia's hot climate (>45°C in summer), the recommended thickness is 150mm to 200mm of PUF panel with a density of 40 kg/m³. Thinner panels lead to high electricity consumption and failure to reach the required temperature. For the floor, we recommend adding 50mm XPS under the concrete to prevent cold loss to the ground.
How is the required cooling capacity of a cold room calculated?+
Heat load calculation includes: 1) Transmission through walls/ceiling/floor (based on K-value), 2) Product load (type, quantity, initial temperature), 3) Lighting and equipment load, 4) People load (300W per person), 5) Door opening load (5–15% of total), 6) Safety factor of 20%. Example: a 10×10×4m room for poultry cooling at +2°C in Dammam requires approximately 25–35 kW.

Sector Solutions

What is the ideal temperature for storing fresh and frozen meat?+
Fresh meat (beef, lamb): 0°C to +2°C with 85% humidity for 7–14 days. Frozen meat: −18°C to −22°C for 6–12 months. Dry-ageing rooms: +1°C to +3°C with 75–80% humidity for 21–45 days for premium beef.
How do I refrigerate vaccines at a health centre?+
Traditional vaccines require +2°C to +8°C in WHO PQS-certified medical refrigerators with a Data Logger, high/low temperature alarms, and a backup emergency system that maintains temperature for 4 hours during power outages. mRNA vaccines (Pfizer) require Ultra-Low −70°C freezers with a Cascade system.
What is a Controlled Atmosphere (CA) system for fruit storage?+
A CA system controls oxygen levels (2–3%) and CO2 levels (1–3%) inside the cold room instead of normal air (21% O2). This slows the fruit's respiration and ripening, extending the shelf life of apples from 3 months to 12 months, and pears from 2 months to 8 months.
What is the best method for freezing fresh fish?+
The best method is the Plate Freezer, where the fish is placed between two metal plates at −40°C and frozen in just 15–25 minutes, preserving cell structure and texture. The alternative is IQF (Individual Quick Freezing) air blast at −35°C for 30–45 minutes. Slow freezing destroys texture and nutritional value.
What is the storage temperature for dates?+
Dry dates (Sukkari, Khalas, Medjool): 0°C to +2°C with 70–75% humidity for 12–24 months. For high-moisture dates intended for export, freezing at −18°C for 24+ months is preferred, fully preserving texture and colour.

Comparisons

Which is better: Bitzer or Copeland compressors?+
Both are among the world's finest compressors. Bitzer (German) excels in heavy industrial applications and ammonia systems, with a longer service life (>80,000 hours) and is best for large capacities (>50 HP). Copeland (American) leads in Scroll technology, quieter operation, better suited for supermarkets and restaurants, and slightly cheaper. The choice depends on the application type and budget. Elfarida Ice is an authorised dealer for both.
Which is better: Turkish Assan panel or Irish Kingspan?+
Assan Panel (Turkish): excellent economic pricing, high quality, K=0.022, most popular in Saudi Arabia, fast spare parts availability. Kingspan (Irish): premium, world's highest efficiency K=0.018 (20% energy saving), FM Global and LEED certifications, 25-year warranty, best for high-end projects. For commercial projects, Assan is more economical. For premium/pharmaceutical projects, Kingspan.
What is the difference between DX and Secondary Loop systems?+
DX (Direct Expansion): refrigerant enters the evaporator directly. Economical, common, large refrigerant charge. Secondary Loop: an intermediate fluid (Glycol/Brine) carries cooling instead of refrigerant. Advantages: 80% reduction in refrigerant charge, higher safety for supermarkets, eco-friendly, easier maintenance. Drawback: 15–20% higher initial cost.
Which is better for Saudi Arabia: R404a or CO2?+
R404a (Freon): suitable for Saudi Arabia's hot climate, moderate cost, common with widely available parts, but GWP=3922 (high environmental impact). CO2 (R744): 100% eco-friendly (GWP=1), the future of refrigeration, but requires Trans-Critical technology for hot climates (Ejector Technology) and a higher initial cost. For large new projects in 2026, we recommend R448A as a middle ground (GWP=1387) or Trans-Critical CO2 for sustainability-focused projects.
