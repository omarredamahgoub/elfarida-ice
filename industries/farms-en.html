Frequently Asked Questions

Customer questions about this sector

What is the CA system in fruit refrigeration? +
Controlled Atmosphere (CA) is a system that regulates oxygen (O2) and carbon dioxide (CO2) ratios inside the cold room, typically 2-3% O2 and 1-3% CO2. This slows ripening and extends fruit shelf life (e.g. apples) from two months up to twelve months.
How are dates preserved for the long term? +
Dates are stored at 0 °C to +2 °C and 70% humidity for 12-24 months while preserving colour and texture. For high-moisture fresh dates (Sukkari, Khalas) freezing at -18 °C is preferred for longer life.
