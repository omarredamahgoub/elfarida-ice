Frequently Asked Questions

Questions About Our Equipment & Brands

What is the difference between BITZER Reciprocating and BITZER Screw compressors?
BITZER Reciprocating (piston) compressors cover capacities from 0.75 to 60 HP and excel at evaporating temperatures as low as -45°C — the first choice for IQF tunnels and deep-freeze cold stores. BITZER Screw compressors handle large loads from 60 to 500+ HP with quieter, continuous operation and minimal vibration, preferred for large logistics warehouses and industrial refrigeration plants across Saudi Arabia.
What is the difference between Copeland Scroll and Copeland Discus compressors?
Copeland Scroll compressors are ideal for commercial and medium-duty refrigeration (-15°C to +10°C) with ultra-quiet operation and a COP reaching 4.2 — used in restaurant refrigerators, hotel cold stores, and pharmaceutical warehouses. Copeland Discus compressors are engineered for very low-temperature applications down to -40°C, deployed in industrial cold stores, IQF freeze tunnels, and ice factories.
Why choose Danfoss EEV over a conventional TXV expansion valve?
The Danfoss EEV controls refrigerant mass flow with ±0.1K precision versus ±2-3K for a conventional TXV. This precision raises overall system efficiency by 10-15%, reduces frost accumulation on evaporators, extends defrost intervals, and decreases compressor cycling — all of which cut electricity consumption. Investment ROI is typically recovered within 12-18 months through lower energy bills in Saudi Arabia's high-electricity-cost environment.
Is Elfarida Ice an official authorized dealer for these brands in Saudi Arabia?
Elfarida Ice is an authorized distributor for these brands in the Eastern Province and across Saudi Arabia. All spare parts are 100% genuine OEM with manufacturer certificates of authenticity. The company provides comprehensive equipment and installation warranties ranging from one to three years depending on the equipment type and contract scope.
Which insulation panel is better for deep-freeze rooms: Assan Panel or Kingspan?
Assan Panel (Turkey) is well-suited for standard cold storage (0°C to -25°C) at 80-100mm thickness with 42 kg/m³ PUR density — excellent value for commercial cold rooms. Kingspan (UK) is preferred for deep industrial freezing (-25°C to -40°C) owing to its lower K-value of 0.022 W/mK versus 0.024 for Assan Panel — meaning superior insulation at less panel thickness and up to 18% energy savings, reducing long-term electricity costs in industrial freezing applications.
