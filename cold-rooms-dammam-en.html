Engineering considerations for Dammam's humid coastal climate

High humidity and salt-laden air in Dammam demand specific material and engineering specifications to ensure a long service life for the cold room:

  • 1 Heresite® anti-corrosion coating on all evaporators and outdoor condensers
  • 2 Stainless steel 304 or 316 frames for doors and bases instead of galvanised steel
  • 3 PVDF/PE pre-coated steel panels to resist oxidation from airborne salts
  • 4 Condenser capacity upsized by a 1.20 factor to compensate condensation efficiency in high humidity
  • 5 Double-insulated drainage system to prevent visible condensation on piping
