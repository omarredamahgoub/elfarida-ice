Who We Are

Al Farida Ice — The First Choice in Industrial Refrigeration

Al Farida Ice is a leading provider of refrigeration and cooling solutions across Saudi Arabia. We specialize in supplying, installing, and maintaining cold rooms, blast freezers, chiller systems, refrigeration doors, and commercial cooling equipment.

With a skilled engineering team and a strong commitment to quality, we deliver reliable, energy-efficient systems that meet the operational needs of a wide range of industries — from food processing and pharmaceuticals to logistics and retail.

Our Solutions

What We Offer

Comprehensive solutions designed to fit every client's needs

Cold Room & Freezer Rooms

Design, supply, and installation of refrigerated and frozen rooms using PU sandwich panels with sliding and hinged doors, plus digital control systems.

  • PU panels in various thicknesses
  • Medium & low temperature refrigeration units
  • Sliding & hinged refrigeration doors
  • Turn-key project delivery

Blast Freezer Systems

High-capacity blast freezing units with COPELAND, Bitzer, and Miraco compressors, designed for intensive industrial freezing applications.

  • Copeland units from 1.5HP to 30HP
  • Bitzer_Miraco units up to 34HP
  • LU-VE & Miraco condensers
  • Custom central units

Commercial Refrigeration

Display cabinets and freezers for supermarkets, restaurants, and retail stores, with energy-saving solutions and intelligent control systems.

  • Supermarket & retail display units
  • Open & closed chiller/freezer cabinets
  • Danfoss / Dixell / Carel controllers
  • Digital temperature monitoring

Spare Parts & Maintenance

Extensive stock of original spare parts for refrigeration systems, with annual maintenance contracts and fast technical support.

  • Danfoss expansion & solenoid valves
  • Friga-Bohn & LU-VE evaporators
  • Schneider control & automation
  • Annual preventive maintenance contracts
