Cold Room Door Types

Doors are critical. Right choice reduces energy loss by up to 70%

Hinged Door

Most common, economical, manual, suitable for small/medium rooms

  • ✓Manual Opening
  • ✓Self-Closing via Hinges
  • ✓Economical Price
  • ✓Easy to Maintain

Sliding Door

For large warehouses, saves space, manual/electric, up to 4×4m

  • ✓Space Saving
  • ✓Heavy Load Capacity
  • ✓Available in Large Sizes
  • ✓Excellent Insulation Seal

Automatic Door

High-traffic facilities, sensor-activated, 0.5-1.0 m/s

  • ✓Fast Open & Close Speed
  • ✓Integrated Air Curtain
  • ✓Sensor-Based Safety
  • ✓Reduced Energy Loss

High-Speed Roll-up

Opens in 1-2 seconds, for very high-traffic food plants

  • ✓Instant Opening
  • ✓80% Energy Loss Reduction
  • ✓Flexible Plastic Curtains
  • ✓Impact Resistant

IQF / Cold Storage Door

For deep freezing -25°C to -40°C, 150-200mm insulation, heated frame

  • ✓Superior Insulation
  • ✓Heated Frame
  • ✓Double Sealing
  • ✓Thermal Crack Resistant
