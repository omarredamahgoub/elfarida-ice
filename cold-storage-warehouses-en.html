Components of a complete industrial cold storage warehouse

Every successful cold storage warehouse rests on an integrated engineering system that balances refrigeration efficiency, operating safety, and energy consumption:

  • 1 PIR/PUR thermal insulation, 100-200mm thick depending on target temperature
  • 2 Concrete flooring rated for forklift loads and vertical storage racking
  • 3 Multi-tier storage rack systems to maximise storage capacity
  • 4 High-speed doors and air curtains to minimise cold loss during loading
  • 5 Danfoss and CAREL control systems with remote monitoring and real-time alerts
  • 6 Fire alarm and ammonia (NH3) leak detection systems per safety standards

Types of cold storage & freezing warehouses we build

We design and build cold storage warehouses matched to the stored product, from gentle above-zero cooling to extreme deep-freeze temperatures.

Above-zero cold storage

+2°C to +8°C for dairy, vegetables, fruit, and fresh produce.

Freezing warehouses

-18°C to -25°C for meat, poultry, seafood, and frozen products.

Deep-freeze warehouses

Down to -40°C for highly sensitive seafood and pharmaceutical products.

Multi-temperature warehouses

Independently insulated zones with different temperatures inside one building.

Cross-dock warehouses

Refrigerated cross-dock solutions to speed up logistics flow without long-term storage.

Frequently asked questions about cold storage & freezing warehouses

Engineering answers to the most common questions about the cost, timeline, and systems of industrial cold storage and freezing warehouses.

How much does it cost to build an industrial cold storage warehouse? +
Medium-sized industrial cold storage warehouses start from SAR 500,000, and large multi-zone logistics warehouses can exceed several million riyals. The exact cost depends on floor area and storage capacity, target temperature, refrigeration system type (ammonia or freon), and insulation thickness. We provide a free engineering study and detailed quote within 24 hours.
How long does it take to build a large cold storage warehouse? +
Building a medium-sized industrial cold storage warehouse takes 6 to 10 weeks, while large multi-zone logistics warehouses require 3 to 5 months, including engineering study, thermal load calculation, panel manufacturing, refrigeration system installation, and full commissioning before handover.
What is the difference between ammonia and freon systems for large warehouses? +
Ammonia (NH3) systems are typically used in large warehouses for their high thermal efficiency and lower long-term operating costs, but they require strict safety procedures and more technical space. Freon systems are better suited to medium-sized warehouses for ease of installation and maintenance. We help you choose the right system for your project scale and operating budget.
Can a warehouse be designed with more than one temperature zone? +
Yes — we design multi-temperature warehouses with independently insulated zones inside the same building, combining an above-zero cooling section, a freezing section, and a deep-freeze section, each with its own control and refrigeration system. This is the optimal solution for multi-product food distribution companies.
Does Elfarida Ice provide routine maintenance for cold storage warehouses? +
Yes — we provide annual maintenance contracts and 24/7 emergency technical support for all cold storage and freezing warehouses, including inspection of Bitzer and Copeland compressors, calibration of Danfoss and CAREL control systems, ammonia alarm system checks, and maintenance of insulated doors and air curtains to preserve operating efficiency and reduce energy consumption.
